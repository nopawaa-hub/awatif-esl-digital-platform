🌿 The Amazing Malayan Tapir 🌿

The Malayan tapir is a very special animal found in the forests of Malaysia. It has a big black body with a large white patch on its back and sides. This special black-and-white pattern helps it hide from dangerous animals in the dark forest.

Tapirs are herbivores, which means they only eat plants. They love to eat fresh leaves, twigs, and juicy fruits. Tapirs have a long, flexible nose called a snout. They use their strong snout to grab leaves from branches and to smell food from far away.

Sadly, the Malayan tapir is an endangered animal. This is because the forests where they live are being cut down.
